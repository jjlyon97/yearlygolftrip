South Carolina

Myrtle Beach

Eighty courses on sixty miles of coast — the highest-volume golf trip in America, and the cheapest.

The Grand Strand is where American buddies-trip golf was industrialised: package deals, cart girls, and a tee time every seven minutes. Ignore the snobbery — Caledonia and True Blue are excellent, and nowhere else lets twelve people play four rounds without anyone remortgaging.

Sixty miles of coast, roughly eighty courses, and an entire local economy built around getting groups of eight onto a tee sheet efficiently. The snobbery about Myrtle Beach is half-earned — plenty of the courses are forgettable and the package machinery can feel industrial — but the top end is excellent and nowhere else in America delivers four good rounds at this price. Mike Strantz is the reason to come: Caledonia is routed through an old rice plantation under live oaks and is one of the most beautiful golf courses in the country, and True Blue ten minutes away is its wilder, sandier opposite. Book through a package operator rather than course by course, put the two Strantz courses back to back in the middle of the trip, and ignore anyone who tells you this is not a real golf destination.

Getting there

Fly intoMyrtle Beach (MYR), 20 min · Charleston (CHS), 2h

Myrtle Beach has plenty of seasonal direct service and the courses start 20 minutes away.

Access: Entirely public — book through a package operator

Why go

  • Unbeatable cost per round
  • Big groups are the norm, not a problem
  • Two Mike Strantz courses within ten minutes

Watch out for

  • Course quality varies enormously — pick carefully
  • Spring package season gets very crowded

Kiawah Island

The Ocean Course — ten holes on the Atlantic and the hardest resort round in America.

Pete Dye built the Ocean Course for the 1991 Ryder Cup and it has been terrifying resort golfers ever since. The rest of the island offers four gentler courses, proper beach-holiday infrastructure, and Charleston 45 minutes up the road.

Pete Dye was given a strip of barrier island and told to build a Ryder Cup venue in time for 1991, and what he produced is the hardest resort course in America. Ten holes run along the Atlantic and every one of them is exposed — the wind can swing forty degrees between morning and afternoon and change the course by four clubs. Dye raised the entire course so players could see the ocean, which also removed any shelter. It has since hosted two PGA Championships. What makes Kiawah work as a trip rather than an ordeal is everything around it: four gentler courses on the same island, proper beach-holiday infrastructure for anyone not playing, and Charleston forty-five minutes away with one of the best restaurant scenes in the country. Play the Ocean Course on day three, not day one.

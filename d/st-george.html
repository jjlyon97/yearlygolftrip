Utah

St. George

Red rock desert golf on the edge of Zion, at half the price of Scottsdale.

Southern Utah is what Arizona would be if it were quieter and cheaper. Sand Hollow’s clifftop stretch runs along the edge of a red rock canyon and is as spectacular as anything in the desert, and you can play it for well under a Scottsdale rate. Zion is an hour away.

Southern Utah is what Arizona would be if it were quieter, emptier and about half the price. The red rock is the whole point — Navajo sandstone in colours that photograph like a filter has been applied, with black lava flows cutting through it. Sand Hollow is the reason to make the trip: holes 10 through 13 run along the rim of a canyon with a drop on one side and nothing but red rock for miles, and it costs a fraction of a comparable desert round in Scottsdale. Entrada plays across genuine lava fields on the back nine. The town is small, the courses are close together, and Zion National Park is an hour up the road if anyone wants a day away from golf. Summer is unplayable, so aim for spring or autumn.

Getting there

Fly intoSt. George (SGU), 15 min · Las Vegas (LAS), 2h drive

St. George has a small airport; most people fly to Las Vegas and drive two hours up the interstate.

Access: Public and municipal throughout

Why go

  • Sand Hollow’s canyon-edge holes
  • Genuinely cheap for the quality
  • Zion National Park an hour away

Watch out for

  • Brutally hot June to August
  • Small airport — most trips start in Las Vegas

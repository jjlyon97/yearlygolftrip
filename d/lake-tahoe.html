Lake Tahoe

Golf at 6,200 feet with the lake below — the ball goes forever and nobody minds the walk.

Tahoe’s summer is short and glorious. Edgewood runs down to the shoreline on the Nevada side, the Truckee courses sit up among the pines, and the altitude adds ten percent to everything you hit. Pair it with a lake house and it stops being only a golf trip.

Tahoe's golf season is barely five months and that scarcity is part of the appeal. You are playing at over six thousand feet, where the ball carries roughly ten percent further and everyone in the group spends the first round flying greens and refusing to accept why. Edgewood is the headline, on the Nevada shore, finishing with three holes that run along the water and a par 5 that plays straight at the lake. The Truckee courses on the north side are quieter and more wooded — Coyote Moon has no houses on it at all, which in a resort town is close to a miracle. What makes this trip different from the desert ones is that the golf is only half of it: the lake, the hiking and the towns are worth building days around, so a mixed group does well here.

Getting there

Fly intoReno-Tahoe (RNO), 45 min

Reno is 45 minutes from the north shore and well connected. Sacramento and the Bay Area are both drivable.

Access: Mostly public and resort daily-fee

Why go

Watch out for

  • Season is barely five months
  • July and August book out with holidaymakers

St. George · Utah

Red Rock on a Budget

Four desert rounds among red rock canyons for less than one round in Scottsdale.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
8
Local knowledge: Fly into Las Vegas and drive the two hours — it is cheaper and easier than St. George’s own airport. Sand Hollow’s clifftop stretch is holes 10 to 13; take a camera and do not rush them. Zion is an hour away if anyone wants a day off.

Day by day

Day 1 · 13:30

Sky Mountain ↗

Drive up from Vegas, warm up on the cheapest views in Utah.

Stay: St. George hotel

Day 2 · 09:20

Coral Canyon ↗

Red rock and old lava flows.

Stay: Sand Hollow villas

Day 3 · 09:00

Sand Hollow (Championship) ↗

The main event. Holes 10 through 13 run along the canyon rim.

Stay: Sand Hollow villas

Day 4 · 08:00

Entrada at Snow Canyon ↗

Lava fields on the back nine, then the drive back to LAS.

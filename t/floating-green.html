Coeur d’Alene · Idaho

The Floating Green

A boat ride to the first tee, an island green that moves, and pine forest golf up the road.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: The resort course includes a forecaddie and the boat — take the earliest tee time you can, because the lake is glass before ten. Circling Raven is the better golf and half the price; do not skip it because it lacks the gimmick.

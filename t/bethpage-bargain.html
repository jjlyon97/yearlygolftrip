Long Island · New York

Bethpage and the Island

Four rounds on New York State parkland, including the most famous municipal course on earth.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: Bethpage tee times open on a rolling window through the state reservation system and go instantly — set an alarm. The overnight queue in the car park is a real and time-honoured strategy. Shinnecock and National Golf Links are private; admire them from the road.

The quiet one on the property. Land at JFK and go straight there.

Stay: Long Island hotel (near Bethpage)

Day 2 · 08:20

Bethpage Black ↗

The warning sign at the first tee is not a joke. 2025 Ryder Cup venue.

Stay: Long Island hotel (near Bethpage)

Day 4 · 09:00

Montauk Downs ↗

Two hours east to the tip of the island. State park golf, ocean views.

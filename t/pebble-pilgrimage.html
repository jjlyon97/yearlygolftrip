Monterey Peninsula · California

Pebble Beach Pilgrimage

Three resort courses and one glorious municipal, built around the round you have wanted since you started playing.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: Stay on property — it is the only way to guarantee a Pebble tee time, and it lets you book 18 months out. Play Spyglass first while your nerves are still intact, and finish at Pacific Grove for the best-value sunset on the peninsula.

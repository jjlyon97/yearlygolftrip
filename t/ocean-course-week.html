Example trips · Kiawah Island

Kiawah Island · South Carolina

The Ocean Course Week

Three gentle Kiawah rounds and then the hardest resort course in America, with Charleston up the road.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: Build up to the Ocean Course rather than opening with it — it is a much better day when your ball-striking has settled. It is walking-only before noon and the caddie is worth every cent. Cassique is private, so ignore it.

Day by day

Day 1 · 13:00

Osprey Point ↗

Land at CHS, an hour to the island, lagoons and live oaks all afternoon.

Stay: Island villa rental

Day 2 · 09:20

Turtle Point ↗

Three holes right on the beach. Dinner in Charleston.

Stay: Island villa rental

Day 3 · 09:50

The Ocean Course ↗

Ten holes on the Atlantic. Take the caddie and keep the ball low.

Stay: Island villa rental

Day 4 · 08:00

Oak Point ↗

A relaxed last round on the way off the island.

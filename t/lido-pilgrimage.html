Sand Valley · Wisconsin

Sand Valley and The Lido

Four courses on Wisconsin sand, including the hole-for-hole resurrection of golf’s most famous lost course.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: Ask about Lido access when you book the room, not afterwards — it is guest-limited and the answer changes by season. Walk everything; the sand is firm and the caddies here are good company.

Day by day

Day 1 · 13:40

Mammoth Dunes ↗

Enormously wide. A good place to remember you can hit driver.

Stay: Cottages (4–8 guests)

Day 2 · 08:30

Sand Valley ↗

The original Coore & Crenshaw course. Sandbox in the late afternoon.

Stay: Cottages (4–8 guests)

Day 3 · 09:20

The Lido ↗

The main event — Macdonald’s lost masterpiece, rebuilt from survey drawings.

Stay: Cottages (4–8 guests)

Day 4 · 08:00

Sedge Valley ↗

A par 68 that plays like heathland. Then CWA and home.

Gamble Sands · Brewster, Washington

Gamble Sands in Three

Two full courses and the best short course in the country, on sand above the Columbia River.

Days
3 Rounds
3 Nights
2 Group
4
Local knowledge: Stay on site — there is nothing else within half an hour, and the Cabins split well between four. Play QuickSands in the evening with a beer; it is the most fun ninety minutes of the trip and nobody keeps score.

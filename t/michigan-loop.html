Northern Michigan · Traverse City, Michigan

Bluffs, Dunes and The Loop

Five rounds across northern Michigan, finishing on the only reversible course in America.

Days
5 Rounds
5 Nights
4 Group
4
Local knowledge: The Loop runs clockwise one day and anticlockwise the next — check which way it is pointing before you book, then play the direction you have not seen photographs of. Cherry season peaks in July and the towns get busy with it.

Day by day

Day 1 · 14:00

The Highlands (Arthur Hills) ↗

Land at TVC, drive north, easy opener at Boyne.

Stay: Boyne resort lodge

Day 2 · 09:30

Bay Harbor (Links / Quarry) ↗

Clifftop quarry holes above Little Traverse Bay.

Stay: Boyne resort lodge

Day 3 · 10:40

Arcadia Bluffs (Bluffs) ↗

Two hundred feet above Lake Michigan. The reason people come here.

Stay: Traverse City hotel

Day 4 · 08:20

Arcadia Bluffs (South) ↗

Its Chicago Golf Club homage, then a drive east to Roscommon.

Stay: Forest Dunes cottage

Day 5 · 08:00

The Loop (Black / Red) ↗

One plot of land, two golf courses. Then back to TVC.

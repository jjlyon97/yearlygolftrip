Myrtle Beach · South Carolina

The Grand Strand Value Run

Four good rounds at the lowest cost per round in America, with room for eight without anyone complaining.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
8
Local knowledge: Book through a package operator rather than course by course — it is usually far cheaper and they handle the tee sheet. Put the two Strantz courses back to back in the middle of the trip; they are the reason to come.

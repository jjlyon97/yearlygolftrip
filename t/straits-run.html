Straits & River — Kohler in Three

Two Pete Dye championship courses and a warm-up, from one hotel, in 48 hours.

Days
3 Rounds
3 Nights
2 Group
4
Local knowledge: The Straits requires a caddie and walking — book it for the middle day when your legs are freshest. If you can add a fourth day, Erin Hills is an hour south and worth the detour.

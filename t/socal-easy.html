Example trips · San Diego

San Diego · California

San Diego, the Easy One

Four rounds including a US Open course, and nobody has to drive more than forty minutes.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: Torrey Pines South tee times open on a rolling window and vanish immediately — set an alarm, or book the resort package at the Lodge which includes guaranteed access. Play the North first; it is the better warm-up and the better views.

Day by day

Day 1 · 13:30

Coronado Municipal ↗

Land at SAN, twenty minutes over the bridge. Bay views for muni money.

Stay: La Jolla / Del Mar hotel

Day 2 · 09:20

Torrey Pines (North) ↗

Clifftop warm-up. Lunch in La Jolla after.

Stay: La Jolla / Del Mar hotel

Day 3 · 08:40

Torrey Pines (South) ↗

The main event. Two US Opens have been won here.

Stay: La Jolla / Del Mar hotel

Day 4 · 08:00

Maderas ↗

Canyon golf inland, then a late flight home.

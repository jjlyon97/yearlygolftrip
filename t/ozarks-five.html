Payne’s Valley · Big Cedar Lodge, Missouri

The Ozarks Architect Tour

Tiger, Coore & Crenshaw, Fazio and Player in four days, all on one property.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
4
Local knowledge: Ozarks National is the best golf here and Payne’s Valley is the best day out — play them on consecutive mornings and argue about it over dinner. The cave hole after Payne’s Valley’s 18th is not a real hole and is absolutely worth doing.

Day by day

Day 1 · 13:20

Buffalo Ridge Springs ↗

Land at Springfield, drive an hour. Fazio, with bison in the fields.

Stay: Big Cedar Lodge

Day 2 · 09:00

Ozarks National ↗

Coore & Crenshaw across the ridges. The connoisseur’s round.

Stay: Big Cedar Lodge

Day 3 · 09:40

Payne’s Valley ↗

Tiger’s first public design, finishing in a limestone cave.

Stay: Big Cedar Lodge

Day 4 · 08:30

Mountain Top ↗

Thirteen short holes on foot, then home.

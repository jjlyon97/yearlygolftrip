Example trips · Lake Tahoe

Lake Tahoe · California & Nevada

Tahoe in Summer

Four mountain rounds at 6,200 feet, with a lake to jump in afterwards.

Days
5 Rounds
5 Nights
4 Group
4
Local knowledge: Everything flies about ten percent further up here — take a club less all week and stop being surprised. Book Edgewood for a morning; the afternoon wind comes up the lake and the closing three holes stop being fun.

Day by day

Day 1 · 14:00

Gray’s Crossing ↗

Fly into Reno, 45 minutes up. High, open and a gentle start.

Stay: Truckee / Northstar hotel

Day 2 · 09:30

Coyote Moon ↗

Not a single house on the property. All pines and granite.

Stay: Truckee / Northstar hotel

Day 3 · 09:00

Old Greenwood ↗

Nicklaus among the tall trees. Afternoon on the lake.

Stay: Truckee / Northstar hotel

Day 4 · 08:20

Edgewood Tahoe ↗

Round the south shore. The last three holes run along the water.

Stay: Edgewood Tahoe Lodge

Day 5 · 08:00

Incline Village (Championship) ↗

High above the north shore, then back to Reno.

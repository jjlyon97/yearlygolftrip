Robert Trent Jones Trail · Alabama

The Trail in Four

Four big Robert Trent Jones courses across Alabama for less than one round at Pebble.

Days
4 Rounds
4 Nights
3 Group
8
Local knowledge: Buy the trail card before you go. Grand National’s Lake and Links courses are the two best on the whole trail and they share a clubhouse — play them back to back rather than splitting them up.

Day by day

Day 1 · 13:20

Oxmoor Valley (Ridge) ↗

Land at BHM, twenty minutes out. Severe elevation change to open.

Stay: Renaissance Ross Bridge

Day 2 · 09:00

Ross Bridge ↗

One of the longest courses in the world. Play it forward.

Stay: Renaissance Ross Bridge

Day 3 · 09:40

Grand National (Lake) ↗

Ninety minutes to Opelika. Half the holes touch the water.

Stay: Marriott Grand National

Day 4 · 08:00

Grand National (Links) ↗

Its equal, most days. Then ATL or BHM for the flight.

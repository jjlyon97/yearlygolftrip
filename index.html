How it works

Three steps, one shareable link

Everything is stored in your browser and packed into the URL when you share, so there is nothing to sign up for.

1

Pick where

Twenty US golf areas with the courses worth playing, the best months, how hard each one is to reach and which airport you actually fly into.

2

Build the days

Start from a ready-made itinerary or a blank slate. Add a day, choose a course and a tee time, attach lodging — every course links out for live tee times.

3

Copy one link. The whole itinerary travels inside it — no logins, no app, nothing for your mates to install before they can look at it.

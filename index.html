How it works

Three steps, one shareable link

There's nothing to sign up for. Your trip is saved in your browser, and sharing puts the whole thing in the link.

1

Pick where

Twenty-six US golf areas with the courses worth playing, the best months, how hard each one is to reach and which airport you actually fly into.

2

Build the days

Add a day, pick a course and a tee time, add where you're staying. Each course links to its own site so you can book.

3

Send it to the group

Copy one link and send it. Your friends open it and see the plan. No account, no app to install.
